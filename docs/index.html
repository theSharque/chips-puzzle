What we collect

We do not collect personal data. The app does not require an account, login, email, or any identifier. It has no internet permission and does not send data to us or to advertising, analytics, or other third-party services.

What stays on your device

The game stores only the current level and score on the device (Android SharedPreferences) so progress can continue after you close the app. This data never leaves the app except as described below.

Android backup

If you enabled backup in Android system settings, the operating system may include that local progress in your own Google account device backup. We do not receive or access that backup.

Children

The app is a casual puzzle and does not collect data from children or anyone else.
